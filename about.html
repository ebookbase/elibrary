Class 9 Digital Portal Initiative

OPEN-SOURCE ACADEMIC ARCHIVE

The Class 9 Digital Portal is an open-access educational initiative engineered to provide 9th-grade secondary students with free, lightning-fast, and distraction-free access to all essential NCERT textbooks, chapter modules, and academic literature. Built strictly with modern serverless technologies, we eliminate paywalls, advertisements, and tracking bloat.

Zero Advertisements

Completely free from commercial banner ads, popups, or sponsored tracking scripts.

Privacy First

Client-side cryptographic passphrase verification ensures zero plaintext credential leakage.

Instant Distribution

High-speed global content delivery powered by GitHub Pages static infrastructure.

Syllabus Compliance

100% aligned with prescribed CBSE and National Education Policy (NEP) guidelines.
