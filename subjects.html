Curriculum & Academic Structure

Comprehensive Class 9 syllabus, competency-based learning pathways, and open-source study materials built upon the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) framework.

NEP 2023 Secondary Stage Vision (Grades 9–12)

COMPETENCY-BASED FRAMEWORK

The National Education Policy (NEP 2023) transforms the Class 9 curriculum from rote memorization to experiential, inquiry-driven, and multidisciplinary education. Students are encouraged to develop critical thinking, digital literacy, and holistic life values without rigid separations between Sciences, Arts, and Vocational fields.

Conceptual Mastery

Emphasis on fundamental understanding and application over rote exam preparation.

Multidisciplinary Choice

Integration of technical skills, art appreciation, and fitness alongside core sciences.

Computational Thinking

Early induction of IT, algorithms, cyber ethics, and digital creation tools.

Holistic Development

Structured physical education, emotional resilience, and ethics for civic awareness.
